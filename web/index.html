The game

A dungeon that hits back

Combat is strike-based, not bounce-based. Traversal is precise. The whole 16-bit dungeon fits on a phone — and runs in your browser.

  • Strike, don't stomp

    A committed sword arc, five damage a clean hit.

  • Daggers cost ammo

    Two and a half damage a throw. Find more daggers, or drink Fire Breath and make them count.

  • Double jump

    Chain a double jump and drop through a one-way platform.

  • Seven potions

    Cycle with Z, drink with C.

  • Coins are your score

    No shop to spend them in — the tally at the end is the point.
