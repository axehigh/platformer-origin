The game

A dungeon that hits back

Combat is strike-based, not bounce-based. Traversal is precise. The whole 16-bit dungeon fits on a phone — and runs in your browser.

  • Strike, don't stomp

    A committed sword arc, five damage a clean hit.

  • Daggers cost ammo

    Two and a half damage a throw. Find more daggers, or drink Fire Breath and make them count.

  • Double jump, wall climb

    Chain a double jump, scale a wall, drop through a one-way platform.

  • Seven potions

    Cycle with Z, drink with C. Five slots of each kind.

  • Coins are your score

    No shop to spend them in — the tally at the end is the point.

  • Make your own screenshots

    Press P mid-run and the exact frame lands on your device.

The dungeon

Three depths, one way down

Each dungeon is a continuous run of rooms chained through its exit gate. Finish one to open the next. Clear the last one and you win the game.

  1. 1

    Dungeon 1

    10 rooms·Where it starts

    Ten rooms, end to end, no save points. This is where you learn that a swing has weight and a fall costs a heart.

  2. 2

    Dungeon 2

    11 rooms·Including a branch

    The longest run in the game, and it even has a detour — Level 1b hangs off the first room if you know to look.

  3. 3

    Dungeon 3

    3 rooms·Good luck

    Three rooms. Everything at once. Clear it and the victory screen is waiting at the far end.

  4. ↳

    Tutorial

    3 rooms·Learn the verbs

    Move, jump, swing, climb, drink — three rooms that teach the game before the first real enemy shows up.

Three tries per dungeon

Die and the Game Over screen offers Continue — it restarts the room you died in and costs one try. Burn all three and the only option left is Retry World: back to the dungeon's first room, three tries again, everything you collected still on your back.

Bestiary

Who lives down here

Four archetypes. Health shown is what it takes to put one down with a full sword.

  • Goblin

    10 HP

    Walks at you. Dies to two hits.

  • Mosquito

    5 HP

    Ignores walls, ignores floors, follows you.

  • Spider

    10 HP

    Holds its ground and spits venom down a line.

  • Knight

    15 HP

    The same goblin, half again as big and twice as committed.

Alchemy

Seven bottles, five slots each

  • Healing

  • Strength

  • Speed

  • Invulnerability

  • Invisibility

  • Jump

  • Fire Breath

    Daggers become fireballs.
